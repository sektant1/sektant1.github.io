eleven of us, ten slots, and every night the same argument about who sits out.

![sorteador dos crias cover: the title, a drawn 5x5 with orange and blue teams, a split ct/tr coin and the console command for de_inferno](/content-assets/projects/sorteador-dos-crias/assets/cover.webp)

## Briefing

```text
TARGET      windows 10 / 11
RUNTIME     .net framework 4, ships with windows
BUILD       csc.exe from the framework, no sdk
SIZE        one exe, no installer
MODES       players, 3x3, 4x4, 5x5
MAPS        veto md1, veto md3, random draw
```

## Who plays

the player list is the group, not tonight. click a name to mark them off for
the night; they stay on the list and the draw skips them. the counter reads
`10 de 11 on`.

whoever sat out the last **registered** match gets a ★ and a guaranteed slot
in the next one. drawing again before you play does not move the queue, so a
reroll cannot push someone out twice.

![the main window: player chips with one marked off and two starred, a 4x4 draw in orange and blue, and the buttons to register the winner](/content-assets/projects/sorteador-dos-crias/assets/teams.png)

pairs that must never share a team go in **nunca no mesmo time**. the default
is `felps ≠ vitao`. in 25 test draws of 5x5 they both played in 18 and landed
on the same side in none.

## Maps

the pool starts with ancient, anubis, dust2, inferno, mirage, nuke and train;
overpass, vertigo, office and italy are there switched off. custom maps can be
added. the pool is saved beside the exe.

- **veto md1:** teams alternate bans until one map is left. a coin decides who
  starts.
- **veto md3:** ban, ban, pick, pick, ban, ban, decider.
- **sortear mapa:** a slowing spin over the active pool.

![an md3 veto: two picks, four bans and the decider ancient](/content-assets/projects/sorteador-dos-crias/assets/veto-md3.png)

once a map is decided, **copiar comando do console** puts this on the
clipboard:

```text
game_type 0; game_mode 1; map de_inferno
```

paste it into the cs2 console and the map loads in offline competitive.

![a random map draw that landed on inferno, with the console command button lit](/content-assets/projects/sorteador-dos-crias/assets/map-draw.png)

## Sides

the coin flips for team a; team b takes the other side. the emblems are drawn
for this app, not taken from the game. drop a `ct.png` and `tr.png` next to the
exe to replace them.

![the coin after a flip, showing the tr face](/content-assets/projects/sorteador-dos-crias/assets/coin.png)

## Score

**time a venceu** and **time b venceu** register the match. the history keeps
wins, games, win rate and how many times each player sat out, plus the last
four matches. **copiar pro discord** formats the teams, sides, bench and map
for the group chat.

![the history overlay: a ranking by wins and the four most recent matches](/content-assets/projects/sorteador-dos-crias/assets/history.png)

## Files

everything is plain text beside the exe, so a backup is a folder copy.

```text
jogadores.txt    the group; a leading # means off tonight
separados.txt    never-same-team pairs, one per line
mapas.txt        the map pool; a leading # means switched off
historico.txt    one registered match per line
```

the [build write-up](/posts/building-sorteador-dos-crias) covers how it is
compiled and why windows still warns about it.
